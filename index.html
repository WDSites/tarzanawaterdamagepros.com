Water damage restoration in Tarzana: what it covers

Water damage restoration is the full process of stopping water, removing it, drying the structure and rebuilding what could not be saved. In Tarzana, ZIP 91356, that process usually begins with a call about a pipe, an appliance, a roof or a storm. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and the first conversation is about safety and the source of the water.

The work follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, which describes water categories, drying classes and monitoring. It is reasonable to ask any restoration company whether its crews work to that standard.

Tarzana covers about 8.8 square miles of the San Fernando Valley, with Victory Boulevard on the north, Lindley Avenue on the east, Corbin Avenue on the west and Topanga State Park on the south. That layout shapes the calls. Homes near the foothills deal with runoff, and homes along the Ventura Boulevard and Oxnard Street corridors deal mostly with plumbing and appliance failures.

Which water are you dealing with?

The category of water decides what can be saved. This table follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 description, and a crew classifies your loss on site.

CategoryWhere it comes fromWhat it means for your home
Category 1A broken supply line, a faucet or a roof leak that is just startingOften the most savable, if extraction and drying start within the first day or two
Category 2Washer or dishwasher discharge, an overflowing sinkCleaning and treatment are added, and wet porous materials are usually removed
Category 3Sewage backup, river or storm floodingProtective equipment, removal of porous materials and treatment are all required. See flood damage restoration

Category can worsen if water sits, so a Category 1 leak ignored for days may be handled as a bigger job.

What drives the cost of a water loss

Restoration is priced from the facts of the loss. Phone numbers are never exact, because the size of the loss is not known until moisture is mapped.

  • Water categoryClean, gray and contaminated water need different protective and treatment steps.
  • Area affectedSquare footage and how far the water traveled set the drying equipment.
  • Materials involvedCarpet, hardwood, tile and drywall dry differently, and some must be removed.
  • Drying daysMore equipment for more days raises the total.
  • Rebuild scopeMitigation ends at dry. Repairs are a separate scope, covered under water damage repair.

Local detail

Tarzana in brief, and why it matters when water hits

Author Edgar Rice Burroughs bought a tract here on March 1, 1919 and called it Tarzana Ranch, naming the place for his jungle hero. He later subdivided and sold the land for homes, which means the housing stock spans many building years. Older plumbing and older materials turn up in repairs, so a crew should ask about the age of your pipes and finishes.

Los Angeles Department of Water and Power supplies Tarzana, and its published water quality report lists the San Fernando Valley supply as a mix of Los Angeles Aqueduct water, local groundwater and State Water Project water. For a restoration call, the practical point is the shutoff. A water main break on Topeka Drive in October 2013 left as many as 25 homes without water, a reminder to know where your own valve is before an emergency.

The Encino-Tarzana Branch Library on Ventura Boulevard, Caballero Canyon on the north side of the Santa Monica Mountains and Topanga State Park to the south are landmarks most residents know. Homes backing onto the foothills near them are the ones to watch in winter storms, since runoff arrives from slopes above.

What to have ready when you call

A few answers help dispatch plan the response before anyone arrives.

  • Your address and the nearest cross streets.
  • Where the water came from and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms and materials are wet, and how long they have been wet.
  • Whether water has reached outlets, a panel or appliances.
  • Your insurer's name, if you plan to make a claim, and whether you have started a claim file.

Before you call

If you smell gas, see sparks or hear electrical buzzing near water, leave and call 911 and the utility first. If the water is sewage or flood water, keep people and pets out of it.

What happens next

Dispatch will give you safety steps, ask about the damage and arrange a crew. Same-day response is offered when crews are available. Arrival time is not guaranteed, so ask what to expect for your address.

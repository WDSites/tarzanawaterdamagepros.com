Northridge at a glance

Where it sits

Northridge lies north of Tarzana and Reseda. Porter Ranch and Granada Hills are to its north, and Winnetka and Chatsworth are to its west.

ZIP codes

The campus and the rental market around it

California State University, Northridge opened in 1956 as the San Fernando Valley Campus on Nordhoff Street and Etiwanda Avenue. Enrollment was almost 40,000 by fall 2016, and the campus has its own ZIP, 91330. A large student population keeps a strong rental market in the surrounding blocks.

Rental houses and apartments bring a repeat pattern. A tenant finds a leak and the landlord needs to be told quickly. Document everything with photos and dates, report in writing, and call us if the water is active. The sooner it is reported, the sooner drying begins.

When water is spreading in Northridge

An active leak is an emergency in a house or an apartment. Shut off the water source you control and keep clear of wet outlets. If there is no shutoff you can reach, call the landlord or manager at once and then call the line.

Describe what the water has touched. In older apartment buildings, water can travel between units along pipe chases, and a crew can use meters to see how far.

The earthquake that took Northridge's name

The January 17, 1994 earthquake was named for Northridge from early epicenter estimates, though later work placed it in neighboring Reseda. It was a magnitude 6.7 event on a blind thrust fault, and freeways collapsed. Northridge Hospital Medical Center treated more than 1,000 patients in the first few days.

Water and gas lines were broken across the area in that quake. A home repaired after 1994 may have patched lines in walls or slabs. If you are buying a Northridge house, ask for records of plumbing repairs and have the lines inspected.

Postwar tracts and what they hold today

Settlement here began with Tongva villages and Spanish-era exploration. Agricultural lots were later subdivided into suburban tracts after World War II to meet veterans' housing demand, and commercial development followed in the 1950s. The area was called Zelzah in 1910 and North Los Angeles in 1929 before becoming Northridge Village in 1938.

If your house sits on a slab foundation, a leak is hard to see because the pipes run under the concrete. Warm spots on the floor, a rising water bill or the sound of running water when everything is off are worth acting on. Ask a plumber to check before the floor is damaged. Once water has spread, professional drying follows ANSI/IICRC S500 and relies on moisture readings.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading in Northridge

Stop the source

Close the main valve. In a rental, tell the landlord and ask for the building shutoff if you cannot find it.

Power and safety

Keep clear of wet outlets and fixtures. If the panel is dry and reachable, cut power to the wet area. For sparking or gas, leave and call 911.

Lift and protect

Move belongings off the floor, especially electronics, books and anything that wicks water.

Stay out of dirty water

If the water is from a drain, toilet or outside flooding, keep people and pets out.

Call the 24/7 line

Roof leak water damage is what happens after rain finds a way through the roof and soaks the layers underneath. The shingles or tile may look fine from the street. The harm shows up as a brown ring on a ceiling, wet insulation in the attic or a drip into a closet.

Our dispatch line is answered 24/7. A crew dries the wet materials and keeps the damage from spreading, and a roofer closes the opening. We do not repair or replace roofs, and we say so on the first call so you can line up a roofer at the same time.

How rain gets into a Tarzana house

Most leaks start at a weak point, not across a whole roof. Flashing around a chimney or a vent pipe cracks, a tile slips, a skylight seal ages or a valley between two roof planes fills with debris. Water then runs along a rafter and drops somewhere far from the actual hole.

Clogged gutters make it worse. The Los Angeles County Office of Emergency Management storm-season guide tells residents to clear outdoor drains and gutters of leaves, dirt and debris before storms. Water that cannot leave through a gutter backs up under the roof edge and into the soffit.

Intense rain tests every one of those weak points at once. According to a National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Weather Prediction Center summary, the atmospheric river of February 4 to 6, 2024 brought about 11 inches of rain to Woodland Hills, next door to Tarzana, in roughly two and a half days. The same summary warned of locally catastrophic flash and urban flooding across Los Angeles.

What you see and what it usually means

Ceilings and attics report the problem in a few familiar ways. This table pairs each sign with the most likely cause and the sensible next step.

Signs overlap, so a crew confirms the source with a moisture meter instead of guessing from the stain.

First-hour safety steps

A roof leak is slow compared with a burst pipe, but the first hour still matters because wet drywall is heavy and wiring may be involved.

  1. Move people and pets out from under any sagging or bulging section of ceiling.
  2. Put a bucket or a plastic tub under the drip and lay towels around it.
  3. Switch off power to any room where water is dripping from a fixture, fan or outlet, but only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.
  4. Move furniture, electronics and rugs out of the wet zone.
  5. Photograph the stain and the drip before anything is moved, then call the line.

Do not climb onto a wet roof. A tarp is a job for a roofer with the right equipment, and a fall is a larger problem than a leak.

A bulging ceiling can fail without warning

If paint is ballooning, water is pooled behind it. A small hole made with a screwdriver at the lowest point lets it drain into a bucket in a controlled way. Stand to the side while you do it, and call us if you would rather not.

Urgent: why a roof leak should not wait for morning

A roof leak becomes urgent when water is still arriving, when it is dripping near lights or outlets or when the ceiling is bulging. Each hour adds water to insulation, which holds it like a sponge and keeps it close to framing.

Slow leaks can wait for a daylight visit as long as the area is protected and the call is made first thing. If you are unsure which kind you have, call and describe it, and dispatch will tell you what to do while you wait.

What the crew does once the roof is covered

Drying follows a set order. A crew finds every wet layer, not just the one that shows a stain, because a dry ceiling can sit under a wet attic.

  1. Scan the ceiling, upper walls and attic access with a thermal camera and a pin meter to trace where water traveled.
  2. Remove saturated insulation and any drywall that has lost strength, usually by cutting back to dry, solid material.
  3. Extract standing water in the attic floor or on the room below with wet-dry vacuums and small extraction units.
  4. Set air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers to dry the cavity and the room.
  5. Return to take readings and move equipment until framing reaches its dry standard.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for this kind of drying. Once the materials are dry, closing the ceiling is covered under drywall water damage repair.

Insurance and a roof leak

The state insurance department's residential claims guide separates flood, which homeowners policies exclude, from other water damage, which they generally cover. Rain that comes in through a roof is not flood in that sense, but how a policy treats a worn roof is a question for your insurer.

The same guide says to report water damage to your agent or company representative immediately. Keep photos, take moisture readings if a crew offers them and keep every receipt. Nobody can promise a claim will be approved.

Mistakes to avoid after a roof leak

A few common choices make the damage worse or the repair harder to prove.

  • Painting over the stain before the cavity above has been dried.
  • Leaving wet insulation in place because the ceiling looks dry from below.
  • Waiting for the next storm to see whether the leak comes back.
  • Throwing away damaged materials before photographing them.
  • Climbing up to find the hole during or just after rain.

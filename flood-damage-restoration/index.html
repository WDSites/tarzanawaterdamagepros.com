Flood damage restoration in Tarzana starts with the assumption that floodwater is contaminated, because it has crossed streets, soil and drains before it reached your house. Our line is answered 24/7. While you wait, stay out of standing water, switch off power only if the panel is dry and easy to reach, and call 911 for any sparking, gas smell or rising water that traps people.

Floods and leaks look similar from the doorway but are handled differently. Flooding means Category 2 or Category 3 water in ANSI/IICRC S500 terms, with protective equipment, removal of porous materials and thorough drying. A clean pipe break is covered under emergency water removal.

When flood water is an emergency

Treat flood water as an emergency whenever it is moving, rising or in contact with electrical systems. Water that has been in a room for more than a day or that came from a drain or a street should be treated as hazardous even if it looks clear.

  • Move people and pets to a dry area and keep them out of the water.
  • Do not walk through standing water to reach a panel, an outlet or a gas appliance.
  • Photograph the water line on walls and furniture before anything is moved.
  • Lift photographs, papers and electronics above the water line if that can be done safely.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe the source, the depth and what rooms are affected.

Why the San Fernando Valley floods the way it does

The Sepulveda Dam, built by the Army Corps of Engineers and completed in 1941, sits in the Valley to hold back winter flood waters along the Los Angeles River. It was built after the 1938 Los Angeles River flood, which killed 144 people according to published accounts. The structure is a reminder that this part of Los Angeles was shaped by flood control.

The basin sits in Encino and Lake Balboa, and Tarzana lies just west of Encino, with the Santa Monica Mountains on its southern edge and the Ventura Boulevard corridor running through its middle. Heavy winter storms are concentrated from roughly November through March, so that is when flooded garages and low rooms are most likely. The practical step is to look up your own address instead of relying on a neighborhood guess.

Check your flood zone and your policy

The FEMA Flood Map Service Center lets you look up the flood zone for a specific address. The California Department of Insurance states that homeowners policies do not cover flood damage and that flood insurance is a separate policy you can buy in addition to a homeowners policy.

Many flood policies have a waiting period before they take effect, so a purchase made after storm warnings are issued may not help with that storm. Ask your agent about timing, and ask what your homeowners policy does cover when water comes from a plumbing failure rather than a flood.

Basement and lower-level flooding cleanup

Water collects in whatever is lowest. Depending on the property, that may be a basement, a below-grade garage, a lower-level family room or a ground-floor room behind a hillside retaining wall. If your home has one of these spaces, it is the first place to check after a storm.

Crews start with extraction using submersible pumps and truck-mounted units, then remove wet carpet, pad and the lower few feet of drywall if the water was contaminated. Concrete and masonry can be cleaned and dried in place. Wood framing gets cleaned, treated and dried before anything is closed up. Restoration is not waterproofing, and a house that floods repeatedly may need a drainage fix from a different trade.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Sewage backups are Category 3 and are treated as hazardous. Stop using water in the house, keep people and pets out of the affected room and do not try to clean it yourself.

In Los Angeles, wastewater travels from buildings through private sewer pipes to public sewers, and LA Sanitation publishes a 24-hour customer care line at 1-800-773-2489 for sewer questions. The city also names backwater valves as an important part of a private sewer system. Call the utility if the problem may be in the public main, and call us for the cleanup inside the house.

Electrical and gas safety comes first

If water has reached an outlet, a panel or a gas appliance, leave the room and call 911 and the utility before you call the restoration line. Never stand in water to operate a breaker.

What the three water categories mean

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard groups water by how contaminated it is, and the category drives what can be saved.

CategoryTypical sourceUsual handling
Category 1A broken supply line or a faucetExtract and dry, most materials can be saved if caught early
Category 2Washing machine or dishwasher dischargeExtract, clean and treat, remove porous materials that stayed wet
Category 3Sewage, river flooding, storm runoffProtective gear, remove porous materials, treat and dry

Category can worsen over time if water sits. A crew classifies the loss on site.

What affects the price of flood restoration

Flood work is priced from depth, duration and contamination.

  • Water categoryContaminated water adds protective gear, disposal and treatment.
  • Depth and durationDeeper and longer exposure raises extraction time and material removal.
  • Materials affectedCarpet, drywall height and cabinets decide how much must be removed.
  • Drying days and equipmentMore wet area needs more air movers and dehumidifiers.
  • Rebuild scopeMitigation ends at dry. Repairs are a separate scope.

Price follows scope, and scope follows what a crew finds on site. If you want a figure for your own rooms, call and describe the damage.

Sherman Oaks at a glance

Where it sits

Sherman Oaks runs from the Santa Monica Mountains north toward the Los Angeles River channel, with Encino to the west and Studio City to the east.

The upstairs leak that wrecks a unit below

In the 2000 census, 58.9 percent of Sherman Oaks households rented. A neighborhood with that many apartments and condos sees a steady stream of leaks that start in one unit and soak another. A washing machine hose, a dishwasher line or a toilet supply line fails upstairs, and the ceiling below stains within hours.

If you are the unit below, tell your manager or association right away, photograph everything and call. If you are the unit above, shut off your water and tell the manager. Both sides should keep a record of dates, photos and who was told.

When water is spreading in Sherman Oaks

A leak between floors is an emergency because water follows the path of least resistance. It runs through light fixtures, along beams and down wall cavities, and the visible stain is rarely the full extent.

Do not turn on a ceiling light that is wet or dripping. Turn off power to the area at the panel only if it is dry and safe to reach, and call. A crew will use meters to find how far the moisture traveled.

Renters, owners and who pays

Who is responsible for a loss depends on the cause, the lease and the policy. Renters should photograph belongings, keep receipts and check their own renters policy, while owners should check their homeowners or condo policy and any association master policy.

The California Department of Insurance advises policyholders to keep an inventory of damaged property and to keep receipts for temporary repairs. A crew's moisture readings and photos can support that record, but insurance decisions belong to your insurer.

The river channel and the hills

Part of Sherman Oaks' northern boundary follows the Los Angeles River, which here is mainly a concrete-lined flood control channel. The neighborhood also climbs into the Santa Monica Mountains, and winter rain, concentrated from roughly November through March, is the season when garage and lower-level flooding calls arrive.

Hillside homes should check retaining walls and drainage before the first storm. Homes near the channel should look up their address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. Storm and runoff calls are handled under flood damage restoration.

A neighborhood with a short, specific history

Sherman Oaks was founded in 1927 and named for General Moses Sherman, a partner in the Los Angeles Suburban Homes Company, which subdivided about 1,000 acres. In 2009 the City Council added about 1,800 homes from Van Nuys, and the neighborhood's boundary has shifted more than once since.

That is useful for one practical reason. Because the boundary moved, a mailing address that says Van Nuys may still be inside Sherman Oaks. If you are unsure whether your address is served, call and give the street.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading in Sherman Oaks

Stop the source

Shut off the water you control. In a condo, ask the manager for the building shutoff.

Power and safety

Do not touch wet fixtures or switches. Cut power only if the panel is dry. Sparking, buzzing or a gas smell means leave and call 911 first.

Lift and protect

Move electronics, papers and soft furnishings out from under the leak.

Stay out of dirty water

Water from a drain, a toilet or a rising channel is contaminated. Keep out of it.

Call the 24/7 line

Call the line with your street, your floor and a short description of the leak.

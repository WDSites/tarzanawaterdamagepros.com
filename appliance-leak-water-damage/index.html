Appliance Leak Water Damage in Tarzana, CA

Hoses split, gaskets age and drain lines clog. Cleanup after an appliance leak depends on how long the water ran and where it went.

Call (818) 337-2642

Appliances fail quietly. A washer hose bulges for months, a dishwasher gasket stiffens, a refrigerator ice-maker line kinks behind the unit, and then one day a floor is wet. Because these machines sit against walls and over cabinets, the water often travels out of sight before anyone notices.

This page covers leaks from washers, dishwashers, refrigerators and their supply and drain lines. A leaking water heater has its own page, and a flooded kitchen as a room is covered under kitchen water damage restoration.

Where appliance leaks come from

Washing machines fail at the two supply hoses behind the unit, at the drain hose and at the pump. Dishwashers leak at the door gasket, the inlet valve, the drain connection and the float switch. Refrigerators with ice makers or water dispensers leak where a thin supply tube connects, usually behind the unit.

In apartments and condominiums, a laundry closet or a dishwasher on an upper floor can put water into the ceiling below. Tell dispatch which floor you are on and whether neighbors are affected.

First-hour safety steps

Water and electricity share these spaces, so safety comes first. Take these steps only if it is safe to do so.

  1. Unplug the appliance only if the outlet and the floor in front of it are dry.
  2. Close the supply valve for that machine, usually on the wall behind it, or close the main valve if you cannot find it.
  3. Stay out of standing water that touches an outlet, a power strip or a cord.
  4. Move rugs, boxes and baskets off the floor and photograph the damage before cleanup.
  5. Call dispatch and say which appliance leaked and which rooms are wet.

Finding a hidden leak with the water meter

Some leaks never show a puddle. LADWP explains that if the leak gauge on your water meter keeps moving while every fixture is off, water is escaping somewhere. Turn off every tap and appliance, then check the gauge. If it moves, a line is leaking somewhere.

If the gauge moves and the appliance valves are the only open lines, close them one at a time and watch the gauge. That narrows the source and helps the plumber and the crew.

Our process after an appliance leak

Crews extract standing water, pull baseboards and check behind and beneath the appliance with a moisture meter and thermal camera. Cabinet toe kicks, the wall cavity behind the machine and the subfloor are all checked, since water likes to collect there.

Air movers and a dehumidifier then dry the area. Hardwood and engineered floors are handled gently to avoid cupping, and wet carpet pad is removed if it cannot be dried in place. Crews return to read moisture and finish when the materials are at the dry standard described in ANSI/IICRC S500. Mold is checked for if the leak was slow.

Slow leaks and mold

The California Department of Insurance notes that water damage showing leakage over a period of time may mean mold has already developed. An appliance that has leaked slowly behind a cabinet fits that pattern. If you see staining or smell mustiness, call before you refinish the area, and see mold remediation if growth is present.

Insurance, licensing and who does what

Insurers treat sudden failures differently from long-term seepage in many policies, so read yours and ask your insurer how an appliance leak is handled. Keep photos, the appliance label and receipts for the cleanup and the repair.

Licensing rules from the state contractor board apply once a job passes a set dollar threshold, so check anyone who will rebuild a cabinet or floor. A plumber or appliance technician repairs the machine, and restoration dries the structure.

Prevention and mistakes to avoid

  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided lines and check them each year.
  • Pull a washer and dishwasher forward occasionally and look for damp spots or staining.
  • Do not leave a washer running when no one is home.
  • Never push a damp cabinet back against the wall and hope it dries.
  • Do not ignore a musty smell near a machine.
